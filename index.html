How to play: In each turn, the shooter secretly picks a lane, then the defender picks where to block. If the lanes match, the defender saves and scores; if they differ, the shot gets through and the shooter scores. The score updates automatically as you make choices. Play locally together and don’t peek at the shooter’s choice!
